About

Tetiana Parker is a botanical artist based in South Jersey. She paints one wildflower per canvas in saturated color and clean graphic line, by hand, with Posca acrylic paint markers. Every piece is a one-of-a-kind original; she does not sell prints.

Her work is made for three kinds of collectors: people buying a small original study for their home on Etsy, interior designers and art advisors commissioning large 60×72 inch canvases, and shops and boutiques stocking her 11×14 originals.

Where to find her work

Studies: tetianaparker.etsy.com, 8×10 to 18×24, free US shipping, new paintings almost every day.
Commissions: The Wildflower Commissions, 60×72 in, $6,800 plus shipping, 15% trade discount.
Wholesale: packs of six 11×14 canvas originals, $385 per pack on Faire (suggested retail $770) or $300 plus shipping when ordered by email, ships within 10 days. Retailers order on Faire or by email at hello@tetianaparker.com.

Wildflowers and garden flowers including sweet pea, cosmos, forget-me-not, bluebell, buttercup, lavender, cornflower, gerbera, oxeye daisy, Iceland poppy, California poppy and red corn poppy.
